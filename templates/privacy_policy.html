Privacy Policy

Rubric AI values your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your data when you use our website and services.

1. Data Collection

  • Types of Data Collected: We collect the following data from users:
    • Email addresses
    • Text inputs from student assignments and rubrics
  • Cookies and Tracking: We do not collect data through cookies or other tracking technologies.

2. Data Usage

  • The collected email address is used for account management and communication purposes.
  • Text inputs from assignments and rubrics are processed temporarily and are not stored.
  • We do not share any user data with third parties.

3. Data Storage and Security

  • Storage: User email addresses are stored securely in a protected database.
  • Security Measures: We implement industry-standard security measures, including encryption and access controls, to safeguard your data.

4. Data Retention

  • Retention Period: User email addresses are retained until a user requests deletion.
  • Data Deletion: Users can request to have their data deleted by contacting us at careplancto@gmail.com.

5. User Rights

  • Access: Users have the right to access the data we hold about them.
  • Correction: Users can request corrections to their data.
  • Deletion: Users may request their data be deleted at any time.
  • How to Exercise Rights: Users can email careplancto@gmail.com to exercise any of these rights.

6. Cookies and Tracking

Rubric AI does not use cookies or other tracking technologies.

7. Third-party Services

We use hosting services to operate our platform. These services do not have their own privacy policies and adhere to Rubric AI’s data security measures.

8. Changes to the Privacy Policy

Rubric AI may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Users will be notified of any changes via email.

9. Contact Information

For privacy-related inquiries or concerns, please contact us at careplancto@gmail.com.
